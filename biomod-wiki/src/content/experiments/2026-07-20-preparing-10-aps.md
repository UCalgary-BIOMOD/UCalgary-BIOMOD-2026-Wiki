## Objective

Make 10% APS for making native PAGE gel.

## Materials

- 0.75 g Ammonium Persulfate (APS)
- 7.5 mL Deionized Water
- 50 mL Conical Tube
- Scoopula
- Weigh Boat
- Scale

## Procedure

1. Added 7.5 mL of deionized water into a 50 mL conical tube.
2. Weighed out 0.75 grams of ammonium persulfate in a weighing boat.
3. Transferred the ammonium persulfate into the same tube containing the deionized water.
4. Mixed thoroughly by inverting and shaking slightly to fully dissolve the powder.
5. Labelled the tube and stored in the fridge; use within a week.

## Notes/Observations

- We weighed out 750 mg of ammonium persulfate.
