## Objective

Qualitatively confirm that methylene blue retains redox activity after covalent conjugation to the aptamer.

## Materials

- Potassium hydroxide
- Glucose (dextrose)
- Aptamer-methylene blue conjugate
- Eppendorf tube

## Procedure

1. Weighed out 2.5 mg of potassium hydroxide and added to the eppendorf tube.
2. Added 100 µL of water and 3 mg of glucose to the eppendorf tube.
    1. Swirled carefully until the solids have dissolved.
3. Introduced 1.5 µL of the aptamer-methylene blue into the eppendorf tube and swirled to distribute the dye.
4. Shook the tube vigorously to dissolve oxygen from the air into the solution.
5. Left the tube undisturbed and recorded any colour changes, including the time taken for blue colouration to appear and subsequently fade.
    1. The blue colour faded to colourless in about 30 seconds.
    2. Lasted around 15-20 cycles.
6. Repeated the shaking and resting sequence and recorded the number of cycles observed.

## Notes/Observations

- The first cycle took ~30 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The second cycle took 44 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The third cycle took 1 minute and 44 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The fourth cycle took 2 minutes and 9 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The fifth cycle took ~40 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The sixth cycle took 1 minute and 2 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The seventh cycle took 1 minute and 22 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The eighth cycle took 2 minutes and 12 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The ninth cycle took 3 minutes and 18 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The tenth cycle took 4 minutes and 14 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The eleventh cycle took 3 minutes and 38 seconds for the solution to be colourless.
- The twelfth cycle took 3 minutes and 53 seconds for the solution to be colourless.

## Results

These observations indicate that the aptamer-MB conjugate functions as expected as it can switch between blue and colourless, or oxidized and reduced.
