## Objective

To stain the TMSD native PAGE gel and image the gel.

## Materials

- 5 µl SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 50 mL 1x TBE Buffer
- Gel From Non-SDS Native PAGE
- Plastic Tray

## Procedure

1. Prepared the staining solution by adding 5 µL of SYBR Safe DNA stain to 50 mL of 1X TBE buffer in a plastic tray and mixing thoroughly.
2. Carefully transfer the native PAGE gel (stored overnight in buffer in the fridge) into the staining solution.
3. Covered the tray with aluminum foil to protect the stain from light exposure.
4. Manually agitated the staining tray every few minutes during the staining process.
5. Stained the gel for approximately 30 minutes at room temperature.
6. Removed the gel from the staining solution and transferred it to the Bento Lab imaging system.
7. Captured fluorescence images of the stained gel.
8. Stored stained gel in the fridge for 45 minutes and re-imaged it.

## Notes/Observations

- There were no bands present when imaging using the blue light.
- Staining the gel for longer does not help.

## Results

**Figure 4.** Native polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis using 15% acrylamide gel solution in 1x TBE buffer and visualized by SYBR Safe staining. DNA samples with a concentration of 10 um were prepared to be loaded into the polyacrylamide gel by mixing 10 μL of the samples with 2 μL of 6x gel loading dye. The gel was run at 120 V for 40 minutes and imaged using the Bento Lab system. No visible fluorescent bands were observed under blue-light illumination.
