## Objective

Make 1x TBE buffer for TMSD native PAGE experiment

## Materials

- 2.033 g Magnesium Chloride Hexahydrate
- 900 mL Deionized Water
- 100 mL 10X TBE
- Weigh Boat
- Scale
- Scoopula
- 15 mL Conical Tube
- 500 mL Graduated Cylinder
- 1 L Bottle

## Procedure

1. Made 1M MgCl₂:
  a. Weighed out 2.033 grams of magnesium chloride hexahydrate in a weighing boat. 
  b. Added 10 mL of deionized water in the 15 mL conical tube.
  c. Transferred the magnesium chloride hexahydrate into the tube containing the deionized water, close, and shook well to dissolve.
2. In a 500 mL graduated cylinder, we measured out 890 mL of deionized water and transferred it into the 1 L bottle.
3. In the same graduated cylinder we measured out 100 mL of 10x TBE, then transferred into the same bottle containing the deionized water.
4. Added in 10 mL 1M MgCl₂ into the same bottle containing the water and 10x TBE.
5. Closed the bottle tightly and mixed thoroughly by inverting and shaking slightly.
6. Labelled the bottle and stored buffer at room temperature in the cabinet.

## Notes/Observations

- We weighed out 2033.5 mg of magnesium chloride hexahydrate.
- When mixing the buffer appeared to be slightly cloudy, but started to settle after a minute.
