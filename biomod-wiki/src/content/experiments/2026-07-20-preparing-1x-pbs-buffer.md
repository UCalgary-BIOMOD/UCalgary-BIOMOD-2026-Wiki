## Materials

- 200 mL 10x PBS
- 1600 mL Deionized Water
- 2.033 g Magnesium Chloride Hexahydrate (MgCl₂/6H₂O)
- 15 mL Conical Tube
- 2 L Glass Bottle
- pH Strip

## Procedure

1. Measured 200 mL of 10x PBS solution and added it to a glass bottle.
2. Added 1600 mL of deionized water to the bottle.
3. Weighed out 2.033 g of magnesium chloride hexahydrate and added it into a 15 mL tube.

    1. Added 10 mL of deionized water to the tube and inverted to mix.

4. Added the MgCl₂ salt to the flask slowly, mixed well until the salt was completely dissolved.

5. Check the pH level using a pH strip.

6. Top off with deionized water to exactly 2 L.

7. Store at room temperature.

## Notes/Observations

- pH was around 7-7.5.
