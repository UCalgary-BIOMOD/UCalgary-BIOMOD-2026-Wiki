## Objective

To make a 10 um dilution of Thiol TMSDY and aptamer+Aβ4 from the 100 um stock solution and 1 um dilution from 10 um stock solution.

## Materials

- 1x TE Buffer pH 7.5
- TMSD Solutions 100 um Stock
- Aptamer Solutions (C6AB42AptTS, AB42AptTS, AB42Apt) 100 um Stock
- Nuclease Free Water
- 1.5 mL Eppendorf Tube

## Procedure

-  10 um solutions:

    1. Poured TE buffer from IDTE into a beaker.
    2. Pipetted 90 uL of the TE buffer into the eppendorf tube.
    3. Thawed thiol TMSDY 100 um solution from the -20°C freezer and vortexed.
    4. Pipetted 10 uL of the thiol TMSDY solution into the eppendorf tube containing the TE buffer.
    5. Vortexed the solution briefly and stored in the -20°C freezer.
    6. Repeat the procedure for all aptamers solutions, using nuclease free water

-  1 um solutions:

    7. Pipetted 90 uL of the TE buffer into the eppendorf tube.
    8. Pipetted 10 uL of the 10 um thiol TMSDY solution into the eppendorf tube containing the TE buffer.
    9. Vortexed the solution briefly and stored in the -20°C freezer.
    10. Repeat the procedure for all aptamers solutions, using nuclease free water
