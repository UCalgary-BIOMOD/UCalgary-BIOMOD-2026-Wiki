## Objective

To stain the control native PAGE gel and image the gel.

## Materials

- 5 µl SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 50 mL 1x TBE Buffer
- Gel From Non-SDS Native PAGE
- Plastic Tray

## Procedure

1. Prepared the staining solution by adding 5 µL of SYBR Safe DNA stain to 50 mL of 1X TBE buffer in a plastic tray and mixing thoroughly.
2. Carefully transfer the native PAGE gel into the staining solution.
3. Covered the tray with aluminum foil to protect the stain from light exposure.
4. Manually agitated the staining tray every few minutes during the staining process.
5. Stained the gel for approximately 15 minutes at room temperature.
6. Removed the gel from the staining solution and transferred it to the Bento Lab imaging system.
7. Captured fluorescence images of the stained gel.

## Results

**Figure 12.** Native polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis using 15% acrylamide gel solution in 1x PBS buffer and visualized by SYBR Safe staining. DNA samples with a concentration of 1.0 um were prepared to be loaded into the polyacrylamide gel by mixing 10 μL of the samples with 2 μL of 6x gel loading dye. The gel was run at 120 V for 40 minutes and imaged using the Bento Lab system. A strong fluorescent band was observed in lane 2 and a faint fluorescent band was observed in lane 4. Lane 1 contains the Thiol TMSDY strand. Lane 2 contains the AB42AptTS strand. Lane 3 contains the TMSDX strand. Lane 4 contains the TMSDS strand. Lane 5 contains the TMSDY strand.
