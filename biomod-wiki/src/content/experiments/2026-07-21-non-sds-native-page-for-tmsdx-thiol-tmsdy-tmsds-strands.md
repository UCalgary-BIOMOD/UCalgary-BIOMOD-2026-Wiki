## Objective

To prepare and run a native PAGE for the TMSD strands, using a TMSDY strand with thiol modification to ensure that the mechanism works appropriately and that the modification has no effect.

## Materials

- 1x PBS Buffer
- 6 mL 30% Acrylamide/Bisacrylamide (29:1)
- Deionized water
- 2.4 mL 5x TBE
- 200 uL 10% APS
- 1.2 uL SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 10 uL TEMED
- TMSDX (invader strand)
- Thiol TMSDY (incumbent/aptamer module strand)
- TMSDS (substrate/anchor strand)
- 2 uL 6x DNA Loading Dye
- PAGE mini-gel assembly
- 2 glass plates
- Gel Comb
- Electrophoresis Power Supply
- 50 mL Conical Tube
- 15 mL Conical Tube
- 1.5 mL Eppendorf Tubes

## Procedure

1. Prepared 1× PBS buffer using protocol 2.
2. Prepared the PAGE apparatus and checked for leakage.

    1. Cleaned glass plates and spacers using deionized water and ethanol.
    2. Dried thoroughly and assembled PAGE casting apparatus.
    3. Assembled one Hoeffer mini-gel (1 mm thickness).
    4. Added Milli-Q water between plates to check for leakage and leave for 20 minutes.
    5. If no leakage is observed then drain the water in the sink and use a kimwipes to dry the casting apparatus as much as possible making sure not to move the glass plates in the process.

3. Gel Preparation.

    1. Prepared 15% acrylamide gel solution according to Table 1 (for one gel) or Table 2 (for two gels).
    2. Prepared 5x TBE by adding 2 mL of deionized water and 2 mL 10x TBE in a 15 mL conical tube.
    3. Added 1.2 uL SYBRSafe for 12mL of gel preparation.
    4. Inverted the tube to mix thoroughly.
    5. Added 10 µL TEMED in the fume hood and quickly inverted the tube to mix.
    6. Quickly pipetted the gel into the casting apparatus using a P1000 pipette.
    7. Inserted the gel comb when done and make sure to wipe if it overflows.
    8. Let the gel polymerize for 30 minutes.

4. Gel Running Setup.

    1. Removed comb and placed gels into the electrophoresis tank.
    2. Filled the tank with 1× PBS buffer until gels and cathode and anode are fully submerged

5. TMSD Experiment Setup.

    1. Prepared samples according to the experiment being performed. **Incubated each sample of Thiol TMSDY and TMSDS for 30 minutes at 25°C BEFORE addition of TMSDX.** Do not incubate the samples with the DNA ladder.
    2. Incubated all samples at 25 °C for 30 minutes.
    3. Added 2 µL of 6× DNA loading dye to each sample before loading and mix gently by pipetting up and down and tapping the tube on the table to get the samples to the bottom.

6. Loaded the samples into the wells, keep track of what samples go where.

    1. Pipetted 6 uL of the sample twice using P10 pipette.

7. Electrophoresis.

    1. Ran the gel at 120 V for ~40 minutes.
    2. Turned off the power supply and discarded the buffer.
    3. Performed post-run staining with SYBR Safe.
