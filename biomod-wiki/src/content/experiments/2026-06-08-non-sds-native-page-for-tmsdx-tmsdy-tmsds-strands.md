## Objective

To prepare and run a native PAGE for the TMSD strands (no modifications) to ensure that the mechanism works appropriately.

## Materials

- 1x TBE Buffer
- 6 mL 30% Acrylamide/Bisacrylamide (29:1)
- Deionized water
- 2.4 mL 5x TBE
- 200 uL 10% APS
- 1.2 uL SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 10 uL TEMED
- TMSDX (invader strand)
- TMSDY (incumbent/aptamer module strand)
- TMSDS (substrate/anchor strand)
- 2 uL 6x DNA Loading Dye
- PAGE mini-gel assembly
- 2 glass plates
- Gel Comb
- Electrophoresis Power Supply
- 50 mL Conical Tube
- 15 mL Conical Tube
- 1.5 mL Eppendorf Tubes

## Procedure

1. Prepared the PAGE apparatus and checked for leakage.

    1. Cleaned glass plates and spacers using deionized water.
    2. Dried thoroughly and assembled PAGE casting apparatus.
    3. Assembled one Hoeffer mini-gel (1 mm thickness).
    4. Added deionized water between plates to check for leakage and left for 20 minutes.
    5. No leakage was observed, so we drained the water in the sink and used kimwipes to dry the casting apparatus as much as possible, making sure not to move the glass plates in the process.
2. Prepared 5x TBE by adding 2 mL of deionized water and 2 mL 10x TBE in a 15 mL conical tube.
3. Gel preparation inside a 50 mL conical tube (one gel).

    1. Prepare 15% acrylamide gel solution according to Table 1
    2. Added 1.2 uL SYBRSafe for 12mL of gel preparation.
    3. Inverted the tube to mix thoroughly.
    4. Added 10 µL TEMED in the fume hood and quickly inverted the tube to mix.
    5. Quickly pipetted the gel into the casting apparatus using a P1000 pipette.
    6. Inserted the gel comb when done and make sure to wipe if it overflows.
    7. Let the gel polymerize for 30 minutes.

4. TMSD experiment setup.

    1. **Incubated each sample of TMSDY and TMSDS for 30 minutes at 25°C BEFORE addition of TMSDX.**
    2. Prepared samples according to Table 3 in the labelled 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes.
    3. Incubated all samples at 25 °C for 30 minutes.
    4. Added 2 µL of 6× DNA loading dye to each sample before loading and mix gently by pipetting up and down and tapping the tube on the table to get the samples to the bottom.

5. Gel running setup.

    1. Removed the comb and placed gel into the electrophoresis tank.
    2. Filled the tank with 1× TBE buffer until gels are fully submerged.

6. Loaded the samples into the wells, keep track of what samples go where.

    1. Pipetted 6 uL of the sample twice using P10 pipette.

7. Electrophoresis and imaging.

    1. Ran the gel at 120 V for ~40 minutes.
    2. Turned off the power supply and discarded the buffer.
    3. Imaged gels immediately using a gel imager (Bento lab).
    4. No bands are visible, so we performed post-run staining with SYBR Safe.

**Table 1.** PAGE% preparation for 12mL. - 10% gel: 4.0 mL 30% acrylamide/bisacrylamide (29:1), 5.4 mL H₂O (Milli-Q), 2.4 mL 5X TBE, 200 µL 10% APS
- 14% gel: 5.6 mL 30% acrylamide/bisacrylamide (29:1), 3.8 mL H₂O (Milli-Q), 2.4 mL 5X TBE, 200 µL 10% APS
- **15% gel: 6.0 mL 30% acrylamide/bisacrylamide (29:1), 3.4 mL H₂O (Milli-Q), 2.4 mL 5X TBE, 200 µL 10% APS**
- 16% gel: 6.4 mL 30% acrylamide/bisacrylamide (29:1), 3 mL H₂O (Milli-Q), 2.4 mL 5X TBE, 200 µL 10% APS

**Table 3.** TMSD Experiment

- Tube 1: TMSDY (10 µL). Single-strand control
- Tube 2: TMSDS (10 µL). Single-strand control
- Tube 3: TMSDX (10 µL). Single-strand control
- Tube 4: TMSDY + TMSDS (5, 5 µL). Pre-form S/Y: incubate 30 mins @ 25 degrees before loading
- Tube 5: TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=0 (start point): Immediately load after TMSDX addition (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 6: TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=5: Wait 5 mins after TMSDX addition to load (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 7: TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=10: Wait 10 mins after TMSDX addition to load (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)

## Notes/Observations

- The first gel polymerized too quickly and did not allow proper gel casting, so we made a second gel.
- The first two wells were not used, they did not polymerize properly.
- We did not microcentrifuge the tubes because the timing was weird, so the senior lab member said that it was okay to pipette the samples up and down and tap the tube.
