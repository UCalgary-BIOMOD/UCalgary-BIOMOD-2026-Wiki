## Objective

To stain the native PAGE gel and image the gel.

## Materials

- 5 µl SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 50 mL 1x TBE Buffer
- Gel From Non-SDS Native PAGE
- Plastic Tray

## Procedure

1. Prepared the staining solution by adding 5 µL of SYBR Safe DNA stain to 50 mL of 1X TBE buffer in a plastic tray and mixing thoroughly.
2. Carefully transfer the native PAGE gel into the staining solution.
3. Covered the tray with aluminum foil to protect the stain from light exposure.
4. Manually agitated the staining tray every few minutes during the staining process.
5. Stained the gel for approximately 15 minutes at room temperature.
6. Removed the gel from the staining solution and transferred it to the Bento Lab imaging system.
7. Captured fluorescence images of the stained gel.

## Results

**Figure 15.** Native polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis using 20% acrylamide gel solution in 1x TBE buffer and visualized by SYBR Safe staining. DNA aptamer samples with a concentration of 10 um incubated with hemoglobin or amyloid-beta 42 proteins were prepared to be loaded into the polyacrylamide gel by mixing the samples with 2x native sample buffer. The gel was run at 100 V for 60 minutes and imaged using the Bento Lab system. A strong fluorescent band was observed in lanes 3, 5, and 8. Lane 1 contains diluted non-target protein (control; 20 µL). Lane contains diluted Aβ42 (control; 20 µL). Lane 3 contains diluted nonspecific aptamer (control; 20 µL). Lane 4 contains diluted aptamer-MB conjugate (control; 20 µL). Lane 5 contains nonspecific aptamer + Aβ42 (negative control; 20 µL). Lane 6 contains aptamer-MB + non-target protein (specificity control; 20 µL). Lane 7 contains aptamer–MB + Aβ42 (target-binding sample; 20 µL). Lane 8 contains the DNA ladder (4 µL, approximately 1 µg). Lane numbers follow the displayed image from left to right, reversing the loading order recorded in the procedure.
