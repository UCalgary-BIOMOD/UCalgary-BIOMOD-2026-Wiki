## Objective

To stain the TMSD native PAGE gel and image the gel.

## Materials

- 5 µl SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 50 mL 1x PBS Buffer
- Gel From Non-SDS Native PAGE
- Plastic Tray

## Procedure

1. Prepared the staining solution by adding 5 µL of SYBR Safe DNA stain to 50 mL of 1x PBS buffer in a plastic tray and mixing thoroughly.
2. Carefully transfer the native PAGE gel into the staining solution.
3. Covered the tray with aluminum foil to protect the stain from light exposure.
4. Manually and gently agitate the staining tray every few minutes  for 30 minutes during the staining process at room temperature.
5. Removed the gel from the staining solution and transferred it to the Bento Lab imaging system.
6. To capture an image, covered the side slits of the orange plastic cover of the Bento imaging system with masking tape and black sharpie to block out light. Then with the room lights dimmed, hover over gel to capture the image.
7. Captured fluorescence images of the stained gel.
8. Discarded stained gel.

## Results

**Figure 6.** Native polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis using 15% acrylamide gel solution in 1x PBS buffer and visualized by SYBR Safe staining. DNA samples with a concentration of 10 um were prepared to be loaded into the polyacrylamide gel by mixing 10 μL of the samples with 2 μL of 6x gel loading dye. The gel was run at 120 V for 40 minutes and imaged using the Bento Lab system. Fluorescent bands observed for all lanes excluding 4, 6, 8, and 10. Lane 1 contains a reaction of TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 10 minutes. Lane 2 contains a reaction of TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 5 minutes. Lane 3 contains a reaction of TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 0 minutes. Lane 4 contains a reaction of TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 10 minutes. Lane 5 contains a reaction of TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 5 minutes. Lane 6 contains a reaction of TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 0 minutes. Lane 7 contains only TMSDY + TMSDS strands. Lane 8 contains the TMSDX strand. Lane 9 contains the TMSDS strand. Lane 10 contains the TMSDY strand.
