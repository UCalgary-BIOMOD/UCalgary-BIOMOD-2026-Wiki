## Objective

To resuspend TMSD and aptamer strands from IDT in TE buffer and have a 100 um stock solution ready.

## Materials

- Aptamer Strands
- TMSD Strands
- 1x TE Buffer pH 7.5 (Nuclease free)

## Procedure

1. Poured 1x TE buffer from IDTE in a beaker.
2. To resuspend the oligonucleotides, checked the nmol (nanomoles) on the label and multiply the value by a factor of 10.

    1. For example, if TMSDX is listed 134 nmol on the tube, then multiply it by 10 to get 1340, which means 1340 uL (1.34 mL) of TE buffer should be added to the tube in order to get 100 uM stock solution.
3. Added 1340 uL of TE buffer to the TMSDX strands.
4. Added 983 uL of TE buffer to the TMSDY strands.
5. Added 56 uL of TE buffer to AB42AptTS strands.
6. Added 754 uL of TE buffer to the TMSDS strands.
7. Added 152 uL of TE buffer to the NSThiolTMSDY strands.
8. Added 306 uL of TE buffer to AB42Apt strands.
9. Added 338 uL of TE buffer to C6AB42AptTS strands.
10. Added 127 uL of TE buffer to the ThiolTMSDY strands.
11. Added 87 uL of TE buffer to the NSC6AB42AptTS strands.
12. Vortexed all the tubes for 30 seconds and stored them in the -20 freezer.

## Notes/Observations

- The film was at the bottom of each tube.
- After vortexing/resuspending the film dissolved.
