## Objective

To make a 10 um dilution of non-specific thiol TMSDY from the 100 um stock solution.

## Materials

- 1x TE Buffer pH 7.5 (Nuclease free)
- Non-specific Thiol TMSDY 100 um Stock
- 1.5 mL Eppendorf Tube

## Procedure

1. Poured TE buffer from IDTE into a beaker.
2. Pipetted 90 uL of the TE buffer into the eppendorf tube.
3. Took out and thawed non-specific thiol TMSDY 100 um solution from the -20°C freezer.

    1. Briefly vortexed after it has fully thawed.

4. Pipetted 10 uL of the thiol TMSDY solution into the eppendorf tube containing the TE buffer.

5. Vortexed the solution briefly and stored in the -20°C freezer.
