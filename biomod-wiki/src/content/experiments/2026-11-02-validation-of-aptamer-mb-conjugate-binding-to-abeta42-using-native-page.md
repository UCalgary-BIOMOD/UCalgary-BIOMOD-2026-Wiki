## Objective

Confirm binding between the aptamer-MB conjugate and Aβ42 using native PAGE electrophoresis to detect mobility shifts (comparing migration patterns of the aptamer+Aβ42 relative to the aptamer and Aβ42 only control lanes).

## Materials

- 25mM Tris; 250 mM glycine (1L 10X native running buffer)
    - Prepared at 10x concentrated stock, diluted 10-fold for use
        - Add approximately 800 mL of ddH2O to a beaker
        - Add 29 g tris base and 144.0 g glycine
        - Stir until fully dissolved
        - Top up the volume to 1 L with ddH2O
        - Store at room temperature
- 2.7 mL of 30% (w/v) acrylamide + 1% (w/v) bis-acrylamide mix
- 1.25 mL of 3.0 M Tris-HCl pH 8.8
    - Weigh out 36.34 g of Tris base powder to make 100 mL 3 M Tris-HCl pH 8.8
        - Add the Tris base to a beaker containing 60-70 mL of deionized water, stir until completely dissolved
        - Place the pH probe into the solution and adjust the pH to 8.8 using concentrated HCl
        - Transfer solution to a volumetric flask and add deionized water to bring the final volume to 100 mL
- 6.1 mL of dH2O
- 100 µL 10% (w/v) Ammonium Persulfate (APS)
    - Dissolve 1.5 g of ammonium persulfate in 15 mL distilled water
    - Store on ice prior to use
- 10 uL Tetramethylenediamine (TEMED)
    - These are the polymerizing reagents for the gel, add this last
- Vertical gel apparatus
- Glass plates and spacers
- Power supply
- Single-stranded DNA 20/100 Ladder
- 0.5 mM Aβ42 solution
    - 1 mg Aβ42
        - Scale has a limit of 0.1 mg → 0.2 mg
        - Soluble in 1 mg/mL
- 0.5 mM Hemoglobin
- Aptamer-MB conjugate
- Nonspecific aptamer
- SYBR Safe DNA stain
- UV transilluminator
- 2X native loading buffer
- Eyedropper or transfer pipette
- Kimwipes or filter paper
- 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes
- Falcon tube
- DMSO

## Procedure

1. Prepared the following working solutions, each containing a final native sample-buffer concentration of 1X.
    1. 40 µL specific aptamer methylene blue conjugate (aptamer_MB) + 40 µL 2X native sample buffer
    2. 30 µL Aβ42 + 30 µL 2X native sample buffer
    3. 40 µL nonspecific aptamer aliquot + 40 µL 2X native sample buffer
    4. 30 µL non target protein + 30 µL 2X native sample buffer
2. Prepared Aβ42 solution by resuspending in DMSO.
