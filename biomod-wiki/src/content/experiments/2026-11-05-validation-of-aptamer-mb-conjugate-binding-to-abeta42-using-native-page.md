## Objective

Confirm binding between the aptamer and Aβ42 using native PAGE electrophoresis to detect mobility shifts (comparing migration patterns of the aptamer+Aβ42 relative to the aptamer and Aβ42 only control lanes).

## Materials

- 25mM Tris; 250 mM glycine (1L 10X native running buffer)
    - Prepared at 10x concentrated stock, diluted 10-fold for use
        - Add approximately 800 mL of ddH2O to a beaker
        - Add 29 g tris base and 144.0 g glycine
        - Stir until fully dissolved
        - Top up the volume to 1 L with ddH2O
        - Store at room temperature
- 2.7 mL of 30% (w/v) acrylamide + 1% (w/v) bis-acrylamide mix
- 1.25 mL of 3.0 M Tris-HCl pH 8.8
    - Weigh out 36.34 g of Tris base powder to make 100 mL 3 M Tris-HCl pH 8.8
        - Add the Tris base to a beaker containing 60-70 mL of deionized water, stir until completely dissolved
        - Place the pH probe into the solution and adjust the pH to 8.8 using concentrated HCl
        - Transfer solution to a volumetric flask and add deionized water to bring the final volume to 100 mL
- 6.1 mL of dH2O
- 100 µL 10% (w/v) Ammonium Persulfate (APS)
    - Dissolve 1.5 g of ammonium persulfate in 15 mL distilled water
    - Store on ice prior to use
- 10 uL Tetramethylenediamine (TEMED)
    - These are the polymerizing reagents for the gel, add this last
- Vertical gel apparatus
- Glass plates and spacers
- Power supply
- Single-stranded DNA 20/100 Ladder
- 0.5 mM Aβ42 solution
- 0.5 mM non target peptide or protein (test specificity of aptamer)
- Aptamer-MB conjugate
- SYBR Safe DNA stain
- UV transilluminator
- 2X native loading buffer
- Eyedropper or transfer pipette
- Kimwipes or filter paper
- 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes
- Falcon tube

## Procedure

### Gel Cassette Assembly

1. Cleaned glass plates with distilled water and then ethanol (in sequence).
2. On a flat surface, assembled the cassette with the shorter plate facing outwards on top of the spacer plate.
3. Slid the glass plates into the casting frame and locked them in place by ensuring that the clip doors are facing outwards.
4. Placed the gel cassette assembly on the gray casting stand gasket and engaged the spring-loaded lever.
5. Placed a pipette tip in between the spring-loaded lever to ensure a tight seal.
6. Using an eyedropper, ensured that the get cassette assembly has no leaks by putting water in between the glass plates.
7. Observed the grey gasket if water is leaking, if no leaks are present, wipe the glass plates using folded kim wipes or filter paper to ensure it is dry.

### Native PAGE Gel Preparation

- Create the acrylamide native PAGE gel. No stacking gel is required, only resolving gel.

1. In a 50 mL Falcon Tube, add 2.67 mL 30% Acrylamide Bis Solution 29:1 [1]
2. Added 1.25 mL of 3 M Tris-HCl solution and brought it to 10 mL with distilled water (dH2O) to make a 8% native PAGE gel.
3. Adding it LAST, added 10 uL TEMED and 100 uL 10% ammonium persulfate and mix the solution carefully.
    1. DO NOT OVERMIX! We do not want air bubbles in our mixture.
4. Immediately poured the solution in between the glass plates.
5. After pouring gel, immediately rinsed flask and leave it in the orange bin
6. Carefully and slowly inserted the comb at a 45o angle to prevent air bubbles from forming.
7. Let the gel set and polymerize (20 – 30 minutes).
8. After setting, removed the gel cassette sandwich from the casting frame.
9. Placed the gel cassette sandwich into the electrode assembly with the short plate facing inwards.
10. On the other side, added a second gel cassette sandwich or plastic buffer dam to create a well in between the two gel cassette sandwiches.
11. Slid gel cassette sandwiches and electrode assembly into the clamping frame.
12. Pressed down the electrode assembly while closing the two cam levers of the clamping frame.
13. Inserted the apparatus into the electrophoresis chamber and prepare to load your samples.

### Electrophoresis and In-gel activity Procedure

1. Thawed 2 x 20 µL (40µL) nonspecific aptamer aliquots on ice.
2. Inside the chamber, filled the wells with 1x native running buffer until it overflowed.
    1. Ensured that there are no leaks!
3. Carefully removed the comb, so as not to break the wells! The gel is flimsy.
4. Outside the chamber, filled up to about halfway to ¾ of the chamber.
5. Prepared the following working solutions, each containing a final native sample-buffer concentration of 1X.
    1. 40 µL specific aptamer methylene blue conjugate (aptamer_MB) + 40 µL 2X native sample buffer
    2. 30 µL Aβ42 + 30 µL 2X native sample buffer
    3. 40 µL nonspecific aptamer aliquot + 40 µL 2X native sample buffer
    4. 30 µL non target protein + 30 µL 2X native sample buffer
6. In a water bath, heated diluted aptamer_MB and diluted nonspecific aptamers to 94ºC for 5 minutes and gradually cool by leaving in the water bath and turning off/unplugging the water bath (~20 mins).
7. Prepared the binding mixtures in separate 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes:
    1. 15 µL diluted aptamer_MB + 15 µL diluted Aβ42
    2. 15 µL diluted aptamer_MB + 15 µL diluted non target protein
    3. 15 µL diluted nonspecific aptamer + 15 µL diluted Aβ42
    4. 15 µL diluted aptamer_MB + 15 µL 1X native sample buffer
    5. 15 µL diluted nonspecific aptamer + 15 µL 1X native sample buffer
8. Incubated all binding mixtures at 37oC for 15 min (allow binding)
9. After incubation, kept solutions on ice (preserve complexes formed) until loading on the gel.
10. Loaded the following wells:
    1. Lane 1: 4 µL DNA ladder (~1 μg)
    2. Lane 2: 20 µL of (aptamer_MB + Aβ42) mixture
    3. Lane 3: 20 µL of (aptamer_MB + non target protein) mixture (specificity control)
    4. Lane 4: 20 µL of (nonspecific aptamer + Aβ42) mixture (negative control)
    5. Lane 5: 20 µL of diluted aptamer_MB (control)
    6. Lane 6: 20 µL of diluted nonspecific aptamers (control)
    7. Lane 7: 20 µL of diluted Aβ42 (control)
    8. Lane 8: 20 µL of diluted non target protein (control)
11. Ran the gel at 100 V for roughly 60 – 70 mins, until the tracking dye reached the bottom of the gel.
12. Carefully removed the gel and immerse into SYBR Safe DNA staining solution
13. Visualized gel using UV transilluminator.

## Sample Calculations

- Agarose Native PAGE Gel % = (2.67 mL * 30% Acrylamide Bis Solution 29:1) / 10 mL total volume = 8%
