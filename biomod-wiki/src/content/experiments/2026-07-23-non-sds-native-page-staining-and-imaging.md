## Objective

To stain the TMSD native PAGE gel and image the gel.

## Materials

- 5 µl SYBRSafe DNA Stain
- 50 mL 1x TBE Buffer
- Gel From Non-SDS Native PAGE
- Plastic Tray

## Procedure

1. Prepared the staining solution by adding 5 µL of SYBR Safe DNA stain to 50 mL of 1x TBE buffer in a plastic tray and mixing thoroughly.
2. Carefully transfer the native PAGE gel into the staining solution.
3. Covered the tray with aluminum foil to protect the stain from light exposure.
4. Manually and gently agitate the staining tray every few minutes  for 30 minutes during the staining process at room temperature.
5. Removed the gel from the staining solution and transferred it to the Bento Lab imaging system.
6. To capture an image, cover the side slits of the orange plastic cover of the Bento imaging system with masking tape and black sharpie to block out light. Then with the room lights dimmed, hover over gel to capture the image.
7. Captured fluorescence images of the stained gel.
8. Discarded stained gel.

## Notes/Observations

- The gel electrophoresis resulted in all the wells running down to the same length, with minimal variation between each DNA sample.
- Well 6 had a slight deformity prior to injecting the DNA samples, resulting in a slight point.
- Well 8 and 10 did not show any results, respectively containing: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (T=0), and Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (T=10). However well 5 and 8, and 7 and 10 held the same DNA samples, where well 5 and 7 did show results.

## Results

**Figure 8.** Native polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis using 15% acrylamide gel solution in 1x PBS buffer and visualized by SYBR Safe staining. DNA samples with a concentration of 10 um were prepared to be loaded into the polyacrylamide gel by mixing 10 μL of the samples with 2 μL of 6x gel loading dye. The gel was run at 120 V for 40 minutes and imaged using the Bento Lab system. Strong fluorescent bands observed for all lanes excluding 8 and 10. Lane 1 contains a reaction of Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 10 minutes. Lane 3 contains a reaction of Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 0 minutes. Lane 4 contains a reaction of Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 10 minutes. Lane 6 contains a reaction of Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS strands with TMSDX incubated for 0 minutes. Lane 7 contains only Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS strands. Lane 8 contains the TMSDX strand. Lane 9 contains the TMSDS strand. Lane 10 contains the Non-specific Thiol TMSDY strand.
