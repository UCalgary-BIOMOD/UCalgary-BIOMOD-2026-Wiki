## Objective

To prepare and run a native PAGE for the TMSD strands, using a non-specific TMSDY strand with thiol modification to ensure that the mechanism works appropriately and that the modification has no effect.

## Materials

- 1x PBS Buffer
- TMSDX (invader strand)
- Non-specific Thiol TMSDY (incumbent/aptamer module strand)
- TMSDS (substrate/anchor strand)
- 2 uL 6x DNA Loading Dye
- PAGE mini-gel assembly
- 2 glass plates
- Gel Comb
- Electrophoresis Power Supply
- 50 mL Conical Tube
- 15 mL Conical Tube
- 1.5 mL Eppendorf Tubes

## Procedure

1. Gel Running Setup.

    1. Remove comb and place gels into the electrophoresis tank.

    2. Fill the tank with 1× PBS buffer until gels and cathode and anode are fully submerged

2. TMSD Experiment Setup.

    1. Prepare samples according to the experiment being performed. **Incubate each sample of Non-specific Thiol TMSDY and TMSDS for 30 minutes at 25°C BEFORE addition of TMSDX.** Do not incubate the samples with the DNA ladder.

    2. Incubated all samples at 25 °C for 30 minutes.

    3. Added 2 µL of 6× DNA loading dye to each sample before loading and mix gently by pipetting up and down and tapping the tube on the table to get the samples to the bottom.

3. Loaded the samples into the wells, keep track of what samples go where.

    1. Pipetted 6 uL of the sample twice using P10 pipette.

4. Electrophoresis.

    1. Ran the gel at 120 V for ~40 minutes.
    2. Turned off the power supply and discarded the buffer.
    3. Performed post-run staining with SYBR Safe.

**Table 3.** TMSD Experiment

- Tube 1: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY (10 µL). Single-strand control
- Tube 2: TMSDS (10 µL). Single-strand control
- Tube 3: TMSDX (10 µL). Single-strand control
- Tube 4: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS (5, 5 µL). Pre-form S/Y: incubate 30 mins @ 25 degrees before loading
- Tube 5: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=0 (start point): Immediately load after TMSDX addition (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 6: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=5: Wait 5 mins after TMSDX addition to load (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 7: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=10: Wait 10 mins after TMSDX addition to load (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 8: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=0 (start point): Immediately load after TMSDX addition (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 9: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=5: Wait 5 mins after TMSDX addition to load (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
- Tube 10: Non-specific Thiol TMSDY + TMSDS + TMSDX (3.33, 3.33, 3.33 µL). T=10: Wait 10 mins after TMSDX addition to load (after 30 min incubation of S/Y)
