This week the wet lab team was in the laboratory conducting native PAGE experiments for the toehold-mediated strand displacement (TMSD) mechanism, however, this time we used a PBS buffer as the running buffer! The team observed that the PBS buffer appeared to be more conductive when the gel was running and it seemed to accelerate the downward shift of DNA strands. We got good visualization of the bands in the gel after staining and imaging, which led us to use the thiol modified TMSDY (incumbent strand) strand and non-specific thiol modified TMSDY strand instead to determine if the modification impacts the TMSD mechanism. We analyzed the gels and determined that the modifications do not impair the function of the TMSD, which means we will be moving forward with the experiments and will be focusing on the aptamer component next! The dry lab team has continued to develop and design the project wiki, design our team t-shirt, and wrote a project abstract for the BURP (Biomedical Engineering Undergraduate Research Program) symposium so that we can present our project!
