This week the wet lab team ran a native gel electrophoresis experiment on the toehold-mediated strand displacement (TMSD) strands to test the strands at a lower concentration to see if we are able to use less DNA strands. The team found that none of the bands are visible on the gel, even after staining the gel for better visualization. The team decided to stick to the original concentration and moved on to running the gel electrophoresis experiments using the aptamer strands in place of the TMSDS strand. The results show that the aptamer is able to successfully bind to the TMSDY and Thiol TMSDY strands. The wet lab team decided to run a gel with all the control strands because they noticed that some gels did not have the control bands present. After staining and imaging the control gel, the TMSDY and TMSDX strands did not show up and the TMSDS strand band was faint and further down the gel, but the aptamer band was clear and present. These results tell us that the TMSD strands are too small and may be running off the gel, but the aptamer strand is long enough to remain in the gel. The dry lab team has continued developing and designing the wiki and the team has settled on a t-shirt design for the competition!
